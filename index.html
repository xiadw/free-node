Why Mangroves Matter

Mangroves are among the most effective natural climate solutions, providing ecological, economic, and social benefits worldwide.

Coastal Protection

Mangroves reduce storm surges and prevent shoreline erosion, acting as natural barriers for coastal communities.

Carbon Sequestration

They store up to four times more carbon than tropical rainforests, playing a crucial role in climate mitigation.

Biodiversity Habitat

Mangrove ecosystems provide nurseries for fish, birds, and countless marine species.
